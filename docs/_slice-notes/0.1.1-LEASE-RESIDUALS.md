- Shared-Mailpit caveat: concurrent users of one configured `ARXIC_MAILPIT_*` sink remain unsafe. Reaping is an explicit recipient-scoped maintenance operation and is never automatic. Mailpit parses `before:` in server-local time while the caller supplies UTC-derived fields; the recipient scope limits any timezone-boundary over/under-delete to that recipient's old mail. Default per-run ephemeral Testcontainers bounds hard-kill leaks by container lifetime.
- Exploration limitation: a multi-kind reversible candidate intentionally carries no `fixtureKind` and skips reversible actions fail-closed. The deprecated single-lease input also silently skips when its lease has no matching `requirement.kind`.
- Mailpit maintenance scope: only `.test`-TLD recipients supplied by an explicit lease, or recipients locally tracked by the adapter, are deleted; real-domain recipients are never deleted. This bounds maintenance safety but means an unrelated fresh process cannot reap a redacted persisted recipient.
- Rehydrate awareness: the pre-existing `renewedExpiry()` retains a persisted expiry that is later than its five-minute renewal floor, including a stale-long value. This narrow behavior is documented here; this slice does not broaden it.
