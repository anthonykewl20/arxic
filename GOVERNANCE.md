# Governance

Arxic is governed by a lightweight maintainer process.

## Maintainers

- `@anthonykewl20` (initial maintainer)

## Decision process

Architectural and policy decisions are made by ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
`docs/arxic-full-adr.md` (ADR-001) is the canonical baseline decision record.

The five-truth-state and evidence-first principles are inviolable and take
precedence over convenience or local optimization.

## Proposing changes

- New work should start in tracked issues and slices.
- Proposals that change architecture or governance should add or update an ADR,
  then be reviewed by the maintainer set.
